# Company logos

These logos identify guests' employers. The respective companies own their marks.

- `lseg.svg`: [LSEG's official website logo](https://www.lseg.com/content/dam/lseg/en_us/images/logos/bespoke/lseg-logo.svg), with excess canvas space trimmed.
- `christie.svg`: the black logo from [Christie's official logo package](https://www.christiedigital.com/about/christie-logos/), converted from EPS to SVG while preserving its proportions and clear space.
- `cadence.svg`: [Cadence Design Systems' wordmark on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cadence-Logo.svg), derived from Cadence's annual report and downloaded unchanged.
- `quantlab.png`: [Quantlab's official website logo](https://www.quantlab.com/assets/images/quantlab-logo.png), downloaded unchanged.
- `christie-icon.png`: [Christie's square website icon](https://www.christiedigital.com/apple-touch-icon.png), downloaded unchanged.
- `quantlab-mark.svg`: the standalone Q mark used in [Quantlab's favicon](https://www.quantlab.com/assets/images/favicon-32x32.png), extracted from the [vector logo on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Quantlab.svg).
